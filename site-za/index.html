        Frequently asked questions

        What people usually ask before they start.

        How does the 7-day free trial work?

        Choose your plan, create your account and verify the card you'll pay with, for R 18,00. Your area opens right away and your first invoice appears on the Billing page with 7 days to be paid, with the R 18,00 already deducted. After that, one invoice per month (or per year on the annual plan), issued 7 days before the end of each cycle.

        Do I need to know how to code, or hire someone?

        No. Add your school's name, logo and colour, create your courses and invite your students, all from the dashboard. Your members area is ready the moment you create it.

        Can I use my school's domain?

        Yes. In Settings, add your domain (for example, members.yourschool.co.za), create the two records the dashboard shows you and the platform confirms it. Until then, your school opens at a platform address.

        How do my students pay for courses?

        Through Payflow, by card, in rand. Link each product to the courses it unlocks: when the sale is paid, the student gets access, or an invitation if they don't have an account yet. If you refund the sale, access closes on its own.

        Do students see the platform's brand?

        Never. The sign-in page, the dashboard, the certificates and the emails carry your school's name, colour and logo.

        What if my school outgrows my plan?

        Talk to us and we'll move you to the next plan. A purchase is never blocked by the limit: a student who has paid always gets in.

        Can I cancel at any time?

        Yes. Cancel in Settings › Billing, without having to talk to anyone. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

        Which currency am I billed in?

        In South African rand (ZAR), by debit or credit card. Monthly plans are billed every 30 days; the annual plan once a year, with 2 months free.

        Which languages do students see the platform in?

        English and Portuguese. Each student chooses their own, and the choice is saved to their account.
